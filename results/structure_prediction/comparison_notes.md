## Structural Alignment with Experimental Structure

I used PyMOL to align the AlphaFold-predicted structure with the experimental 7SFB structure so I could see how similar the two models are overall.

- Number of atoms aligned: 2040
- RMSD: 0.697 Å


The alignment showed a strong overlap between the predicted and experimental structures. Most of the core regions aligned well, while only small deviations were observed in some flexible loop regions.
