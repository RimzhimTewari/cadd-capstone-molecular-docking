# Limitations

## Computational Nature of the Study

This project is based entirely on computational and in silico approaches. The results obtained from molecular docking and ADMET prediction tools are predictive in nature and do not represent experimental validation.

---

## Docking Limitations

- Molecular docking provides an approximate estimation of binding affinity
- Protein structures are treated as relatively rigid during docking
- Docking scores alone cannot confirm biological activity or therapeutic efficacy

---

## ADMET Prediction Limitations

- ADMET properties are based on predictive computational models
- Pharmacokinetic behavior in biological systems may differ from predicted results
- Toxicity predictions require experimental confirmation

---

## Structural and Methodological Constraints

- Only a limited number of ligands were included in this study
- Protein conformational flexibility was not extensively explored
- Molecular dynamics simulations were not performed

---

## Educational Scope

This repository was developed as part of computational drug discovery training and is intended for educational and portfolio purposes.
